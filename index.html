About Anlyway

Anlyway is a semiconductor industry company based in Shenzhen, China — the heart of the global electronics supply chain. Our team works with component manufacturers, distributors and product companies across consumer electronics, smart devices and industrial applications.

We focus on the practical side of the chip business: understanding what a product needs, matching it with the right components, and keeping supply, documentation and communication flowing between factories and customers. Our team of five works daily with datasheets, quotations, lead times and production schedules across Asia and overseas markets.

What we do

Four services that cover the day-to-day needs of companies building electronic products.

Chip & Component Sourcing

Sourcing of integrated circuits and electronic components — MCUs, power management ICs, memory, sensors and connectivity chips — matched to your BOM, budget and production plan.

Supply Chain Services

Quotation comparison, lead-time tracking, order follow-up and shortage mitigation, so production lines are not held up by a single missing part.

Technical Support

Datasheet review, component cross-referencing and replacement suggestions, and technical documentation support between engineering teams and suppliers.

Market & Product Research

Tracking chip market trends, pricing and availability across categories, helping customers plan product roadmaps and purchasing with clearer information.
